\# MAQ Timesheet Interpretation Guide



\## Planned vs Actual Hours



Planned hours represent the expected delivery effort for a project or work period.



Actual hours represent the effort recorded by the delivery team.



Variance Hours is calculated as:



Actual Hours - Planned Hours



A positive variance means the team has consumed more effort than planned.



A negative variance means recorded effort is lower than planned effort.



\## Variance Percentage



Variance percentage indicates how far actual effort deviates from the planned effort.



Large positive variance may indicate:



\- underestimated effort

\- scope growth

\- rework

\- delivery delays

\- unresolved dependencies

\- unexpected technical complexity



Variance alone does not prove that a project is unhealthy. It should be interpreted together with schedule, budget, utilization, milestones, and delivery risks.



\## Utilization



Utilization compares actual effort with planned capacity.



Utilization around 100 percent generally means the recorded effort is close to planned capacity.



Utilization materially above 100 percent can indicate:



\- over-allocation

\- overtime

\- unplanned effort

\- delivery pressure

\- underestimated work



High utilization should be investigated when it persists or occurs together with schedule or budget risk.



\## Pending Approvals



Pending timesheet approvals reduce confidence in the current effort view because the recorded hours might not yet be fully validated.



Managers should review significant pending approvals before making decisions based on current utilization or effort variance.



\## High-Risk Time Entries



High-risk entries indicate that one or more recorded timesheet records have been marked with elevated delivery risk.



High-risk entries should be reviewed together with project schedule, budget, dependencies, and milestone status.



\## Missing Timesheet Data



Missing timesheet data must not be interpreted as zero effort.



If no timesheet entries are available, report that the evidence is unavailable.



Do not assume that missing timesheets mean the project is healthy or under-utilized.
