\# MAQ Delivery Risk Playbook



\## Schedule Risk



A project should be treated as schedule at risk when delivery dates may slip because of unresolved dependencies, delayed approvals, late requirements, unstable source systems, blocked integration work, delayed business sign-off, or unavailable environments.



A project should be treated as behind when current delivery progress materially trails the committed schedule or when a critical milestone is already delayed.



\## Budget Risk



Budget risk can arise when actual effort exceeds planned effort, scope grows without corresponding budget adjustment, delivery delays create additional effort, or sustained over-utilization indicates that the team is consuming more capacity than planned.



\## Dependency Risk



External dependencies should be escalated when they materially affect milestones, integration readiness, data availability, testing, or deployment.



Examples include:



\- ERP integration dependencies

\- unavailable source-system feeds

\- delayed business approvals

\- pending security or access approvals

\- unavailable test or production environments



\## Requirement Risk



Unresolved or frequently changing requirements can cause rework, missed milestones, and budget pressure.



Before UAT or production readiness, delivery teams should confirm that critical requirements are sufficiently stable and approved.



\## Data Source Risk



Unstable or intermittent source data can affect analytics delivery, refresh reliability, validation, and milestone readiness.



Delivery managers should confirm source-system ownership, expected refresh behavior, issue severity, and recovery actions before major release milestones.



\## Recommended Management Response



When delivery risk is identified:



1\. Identify the primary dependency or blocker.

2\. Confirm whether the risk affects schedule, budget, or both.

3\. Escalate external dependencies to the accountable owner.

4\. Protect critical milestones from unnecessary scope changes.

5\. Validate whether effort variance or utilization indicates additional delivery pressure.

6\. Track mitigation actions until the risk is reduced or resolved.
