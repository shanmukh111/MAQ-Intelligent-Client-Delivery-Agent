\# MAQ Power BI Delivery Guidance



\## Common Power BI Delivery Risks



Power BI projects can be affected by risks such as:



\- unstable source systems

\- data quality issues

\- delayed KPI definitions

\- unresolved business requirements

\- gateway or connectivity issues

\- delayed business sign-off

\- late UAT feedback

\- semantic model changes

\- refresh reliability problems



\## Data Readiness



Power BI delivery depends heavily on reliable and validated source data.



Before UAT or production deployment, teams should confirm:



\- required data sources are available

\- refresh processes are stable

\- business definitions are approved

\- data quality issues are understood

\- critical measures have been validated



\## KPI and Requirement Sign-Off



Late KPI definitions or changing business requirements can cause rework in:



\- semantic models

\- DAX measures

\- reports

\- dashboards

\- testing

\- documentation



Delivery managers should prioritize requirement stability before major release milestones.



\## UAT Readiness



Before UAT, teams should confirm:



\- critical report pages are complete

\- required KPIs are implemented

\- source data is available

\- refresh schedules are functioning

\- known issues are documented

\- business testers are identified



\## Schedule Risk



Power BI schedule risk often increases when data dependencies, business approvals, or integration dependencies remain unresolved close to UAT or production deployment.
